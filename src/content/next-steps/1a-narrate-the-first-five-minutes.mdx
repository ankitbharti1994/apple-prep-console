The half of the <a href="/open#timed-and-narrated-committed-day-one-never-once-done">13 Sep exit question</a> with no mechanism behind it — that item's counter carries the tally. Timing was adopted the morning it stopped being an intention and became a rule about <em>when</em>: start the clock before reading. <b>Narration has never been given that</b>, and a bounded version is likelier to start than an open-ended commitment — starting is the entire problem. Five minutes out loud, then stop, on whatever the next problem is.
