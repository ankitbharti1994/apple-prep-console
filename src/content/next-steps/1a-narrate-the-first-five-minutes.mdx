The one discipline still at zero after twenty-one problems, and the only half of the <a href="/open#next-steps">13 Sep exit question</a> that cannot currently be attempted. Timing was adopted the morning it stopped being an intention and became a rule about <em>when</em> — start the clock before reading. Narration has never been given that. <b>A bounded version is likelier to start than an open-ended commitment, and starting is the entire problem.</b> Five minutes out loud, then stop, on whatever the next problem is; five days left.
