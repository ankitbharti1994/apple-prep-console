The one discipline <a href="/open#timed-and-narrated-committed-day-one-never-once-done">still at zero on every problem so far</a>, and the only half of the 13 Sep exit question that cannot currently be attempted. Timing was adopted the morning it stopped being an intention and became a rule about <em>when</em> — start the clock before reading. Narration has never been given that. <b>A bounded version is likelier to start than an open-ended commitment, and starting is the entire problem.</b> Five minutes out loud, then stop, on whatever the next problem is; five days left.
