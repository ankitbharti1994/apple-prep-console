title: 'Fix the testing habit, not the individual tests.'
order: 3
---
Three sessions running the weakness has been in the checking rather than the solving. The capture-list experiment on 27 Aug had the identical flaw as the 26 Aug hash test — two mechanisms live in one program, so it could not distinguish the hypothesis from its alternative. Standing rule before any assertion: <em>would this still pass against a deliberately broken implementation?</em> Build inputs by different routes, add negative controls, and remove one variable at a time. Case C is the template.
