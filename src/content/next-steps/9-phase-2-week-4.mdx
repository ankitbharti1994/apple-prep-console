<a href="/coding">Reverse a Linked List</a> (LC 206), first of three linked-list problems in week 4 — and the topic <a href="/open#coverage-every-problem-inside-topic-1">the coverage audit named as the blocker</a> back on 8 Sep, since phase 3's LRU cache is a hash map plus a doubly linked list and linked lists have never been touched. Week 4 is linked lists ×3 and stacks ×2; week 5 binary search ×2 and trees ×3; week 6 heaps, graphs ×3 and backtracking. <b>Every problem carries all three disciplines</b> — timer started before reading, narrated, fake named before the code — which as of <a href="/sessions/2026-09-13">the checkpoint</a> are demonstrated rather than aspirational. The failure mode is not the schedule: <b>phase 1's coding track was first-class on paper too</b> and produced one topic area out of eleven, because internals kept winning the block. A date slip is absorbed by the 12–16 week runway; a breadth slip is absorbed by nothing.
